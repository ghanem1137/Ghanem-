# Talent Management Program Development

## 1. Align on Strategy and Scope
- **Clarify business goals:** growth targets, capability gaps, culture priorities, and operating model.
- **Define program scope:** roles in focus (critical roles, leadership pipeline, all employees).
- **Set guiding principles:** diversity and inclusion, internal mobility, data-driven decisions, manager accountability.
- **Segment by org size:**
  - **Small orgs:** focus on a few critical roles, lightweight processes.
  - **Mid-size orgs:** formalize core processes and manager enablement.
  - **Large orgs:** build enterprise-wide frameworks and governance.

## 2. Build a Talent Architecture
- **Competency framework:** technical, functional, leadership, and behavioral competencies.
- **Role levels and career paths:** levels, expectations, and mobility routes.
- **Talent segmentation:** critical roles, high-potential cohorts, scarce-skill pools.
- **Governance:** decision rights, cadence for reviews, and data ownership.

## 3. Talent Acquisition (Attract & Select)
- **Workforce planning:** headcount forecasting and capability demand planning.
- **Employer value proposition (EVP):** align messaging with culture and growth opportunities.
- **Sourcing strategy:** internal mobility first, targeted external channels, partnerships.
- **Selection process:** structured interviews, skills assessments, and bias mitigation.
- **Onboarding:** 30/60/90-day plans, buddy systems, and role clarity.

**Adaptation tips:**
- Small orgs can use simple structured interviews and referrals.
- Larger orgs should build standardized scorecards and interview training.

## 4. Talent Development (Grow & Enable)
- **Learning pathways:** role-based learning, leadership programs, and certifications.
- **Experiential development:** rotations, stretch assignments, and cross-functional projects.
- **Coaching and mentoring:** manager coaching, peer mentoring, and executive sponsorship.
- **Succession planning:** identify critical roles, readiness levels, and development actions.

**Adaptation tips:**
- Small orgs can focus on job shadowing and targeted upskilling.
- Large orgs should invest in learning platforms and formal programs.

## 5. Performance Management (Assess & Improve)
- **Goal-setting:** align individual goals with business priorities.
- **Continuous feedback:** regular check-ins and coaching conversations.
- **Performance reviews:** balance outcomes and behaviors.
- **Calibration:** ensure consistency across teams and reduce bias.

**Adaptation tips:**
- Smaller orgs can use quarterly check-ins and simple scorecards.
- Larger orgs should integrate performance data with talent reviews.

## 6. Retention & Engagement (Keep & Motivate)
- **Career growth:** clear mobility paths and internal opportunities.
- **Rewards and recognition:** competitive pay, differentiated rewards, and peer recognition.
- **Manager effectiveness:** train managers in coaching, feedback, and inclusion.
- **Employee experience:** pulse surveys, listening sessions, and action planning.

**Adaptation tips:**
- Small orgs can emphasize culture and flexibility.
- Large orgs should use segmented retention strategies by role and risk.

## 7. Tools & Technology Enablement
- **Core systems:** ATS, HRIS, LMS, and performance platforms.
- **Analytics dashboards:** real-time talent metrics and workforce insights.
- **Workflow automation:** onboarding tasks, learning assignments, and survey cycles.

**Adaptation tips:**
- Small orgs can start with low-cost HRIS and spreadsheet tracking.
- Large orgs should integrate systems and automate data flows.

## 8. Metrics & Success Measurement
- **Acquisition:** time-to-fill, quality of hire, offer acceptance rate, diversity pipeline.
- **Development:** training completion, skill progression, promotion rates.
- **Performance:** goal attainment, distribution of performance ratings, manager feedback quality.
- **Retention:** turnover (overall and regrettable), retention of high performers, internal mobility rate.
- **Engagement:** eNPS, engagement scores, participation in surveys.

**Set targets by org size:**
- Small orgs: focus on a small set of critical metrics.
- Large orgs: build dashboards and set benchmarks by business unit.

## 9. Implementation Roadmap
- **Phase 1 (0–3 months):** define strategy, build frameworks, and pilot in key teams.
- **Phase 2 (3–9 months):** scale acquisition, development, and performance processes.
- **Phase 3 (9–18 months):** optimize retention, analytics, and governance.

## 10. Continuous Improvement
- **Feedback loops:** quarterly reviews of talent outcomes.
- **Program iteration:** refine based on data and leader feedback.
- **Change management:** communicate wins and reinforce manager accountability.

---

### Summary Checklist
- Align talent strategy to business goals.
- Build a competency and role framework.
- Standardize acquisition, development, performance, and retention processes.
- Enable with tools and analytics.
- Measure outcomes and iterate.
